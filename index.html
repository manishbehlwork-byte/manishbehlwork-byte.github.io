PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Built to move less, lose less, last longer.

Every design decision in the NP Series comes back to one question: what happens to this box after five years on a roof in Rajkot, Rajasthan, or Riyadh.

The NP Series adopts a split-body design, which keeps power output high across a wider range of mounting environments than a single-cavity box can manage. A low temperature coefficient limits how much heat affects power delivery, and the horizontal outlet mode reduces wire bending stress at the gland — which also means less cable used per installation.

Internally, the circuit path is kept short and direct to minimize resistive loss, while the diode housings are rated for high overcurrent so the box performs safely with both conventional and high-efficiency modules.

INSTALLATION

Six steps to a bond that holds.

Most warranty claims trace back to installation, not the box. Follow this sequence and the seal will outlast the module.

Clean the bonding surface. Wipe the junction box base and module back-sheet with a clean, alcohol-soaked soft cloth. The bonding area must be dry and free of oil or dirt.

Apply sealant evenly. Run silicone sealant around the junction box base edge groove before pressing it onto the back-sheet, letting it overflow evenly around the perimeter.

Let it cure. Allow at least 5 hours in circulating air before closing the cover. The cover should click into place and not lift when pulled by hand.

Never bridge positive and negative. Connecting the positive and negative leads of the same junction box together will short-circuit and damage the module.

Limit connect/disconnect cycles. Frequent connecting and disconnecting outside of maintenance reduces waterproof sealing performance and service life.

Keep it away from solvents. Petrol, cleaning lubricants, and electronic contact cleaners degrade the housing — keep the box and connectors clear of them.

UNDER THE COVER

What "1500V, IP68" actually means.

Two views into the NP Series: how the bypass circuit protects a string, and how the seal keeps water out for the life of the module.

Three-diode bypass circuit

If one substring is shaded or damaged, its Schottky diode routes current around it instead of through it — protecting the module from hot-spot heating and keeping the rest of the string producing.

Sealed cross-section

Potting sealant surrounds the diode assembly completely before the cover clicks shut, so there's no air gap for moisture to track through — the basis of the IP68 rating.
